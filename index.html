Frequently asked questions about channel managers

What is a hotel channel manager?

A hotel channel manager is a piece of cloud-based technology that allows hotels to sell all their rooms on all their connected booking sites (like Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb) at the exact same time dynamically.

How does a channel manager work?

A channel manager connects your property management system to every booking channel through direct API integrations. Rates, availability, and restrictions you update once are pushed to all channels in real time, and new reservations flow back automatically.

Why does my hotel need a channel manager?

It removes the need to update each booking site by hand, helps prevent overbookings, saves time, and lets you reach more guests and maximize revenue from a single dashboard.

Frequently asked questions about booking engines

What is a hotel booking engine?

A hotel booking engine is a software application used by hospitality properties to secure direct reservations online through their website, social media profiles, and search engines.

Why are direct bookings important?

Direct bookings avoid commissions paid to third-party channels, give you ownership of your guest data and relationships, and let you control pricing, branding, and upsells.

Is the checkout process secure?

Yes. Checkout runs over an encrypted connection with secure payment processing, and guest data is handled in line with GDPR.
